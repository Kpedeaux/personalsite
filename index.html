Kevin Pedeaux

I make coffee and the things that come with it.

I’m Kevin Pedeaux, a coffee roaster and operator in New Orleans. I started Coast Roast Coffee in 2009, opened CR Coffee Shop on Magazine Street, and today I run St. Roch Market. I also build CoreRail and host Coffee with Kevin.

My work has been covered by The Wall Street Journal, WWL-TV, Biz New Orleans, and the Southern Foodways Alliance.
