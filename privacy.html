Privacy

Last updated July 31, 2026

This is my personal site. It collects almost nothing, and here is the whole story.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics to see which pages get read and roughly where visitors come from. It sets cookies that start with _ga. It never shows me your name or exact location. You can block it in your browser or with Google's opt-out tool at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout and the site works fine.

Local storage

The homepage stores one number on your device so the rotating tagline does not repeat itself. It never leaves your browser.

Nothing else

No forms, no accounts, no ad tracking, no third-party fonts. If you email me, I use your address to reply and nothing more. Some browsers send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals; the site does not change what it collects when it sees them, because this page already describes everything it collects.

Questions

Email pedeaux@gmail.com and I will tell you what I have (probably nothing), or delete it.
