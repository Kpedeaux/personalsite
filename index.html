Kevin Pedeaux

I make coffee and the things that come with it.

I’m Kevin Pedeaux, a coffee roaster and business operator in New Orleans. I started Coast Roast Coffee in 2009 selling fresh-roasted beans at local markets, then opened CR Coffee Shop on Magazine Street. I was one of the original vendors at St. Roch Market, the city’s historic food hall, and today I run it. I also build CoreRail, software for food halls and coffee shops, and host Coffee with Kevin, an interview series on YouTube.

My work has been covered by The Wall Street Journal, WWL-TV, Biz New Orleans, and the Southern Foodways Alliance.
